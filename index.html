About Swasthya Sangam

One room for Maharashtra's health community

The doctors who treat, the hospitals that build, the companies that innovate and the officials who enable rarely share a stage. Maharashtra Times created Swasthya Sangam to change that: one day in Mumbai with the Summit, Masterclasses, Expo and Awards under one roof.

Good health is built by many hands. Swasthya Sangam is where they finally meet.

  • 4experiences under one roof

Five themes

Ideas that guide every session

Fewer speeches about healthcare, more answers someone can use on Monday morning.

  • People

    Putting patients, families and caregivers first.

  • Innovation

    New tools, technology and treatment models.

  • Access

    Quality care for every district, not just big cities.

  • Prevention

    Early detection, healthy habits and lifestyle change.

  • Impact

    Results that can be measured and shared.

Frequently asked

Questions about Swasthya Sangam

Cannot find your answer? Contact the team

What is Swasthya Sangam?

A health summit, expo, masterclass and awards event by Maharashtra Times in Mumbai on 30 Jan 2027.

What is the difference between the Basic and Premium pass?

The Basic pass (₹1,499) gives session access only. The Premium pass (₹1,999) adds lunch and access to the networking arena.

Can I attend without a nomination?

Yes. Anyone can register to attend.

Who can nominate?

Any hospital, doctor, brand or organisation that meets the eligibility rules.

Is there a fee to nominate?

Yes. Nomination plans and fees are listed on the Awards page, along with what each plan includes.

How are winners picked?

An independent jury scores each nomination.
