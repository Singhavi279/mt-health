Overview

Celebrating the Best of India's Health and Wellness

The Maharashtra Times Swasthya Sangam Awards 2027 is a healthcare recognition platform celebrating excellence, innovation and meaningful contributions across India's healthcare ecosystem. The awards recognise outstanding contributions from hospitals, doctors, pharmaceutical companies, diagnostic laboratories, healthcare startups, MedTech and HealthTech innovators, wellness brands and public health organisations.

The initiative aims to bring together key stakeholders who are shaping the future of healthcare, while celebrating individuals and organisations making a measurable difference in people's lives.

Eligibility

Eligibility Criteria

Please review the requirements below before submitting a nomination.

Organisation & operational requirements

  • The participating organisation must have a minimum of 3 completed years of continuous, active operations in Maharashtra, India, as of 31 March 2026. This applies to all organisational nominees, including startups, and is measured from the commencement of active operations, not merely the date of incorporation.
  • The participating organisation must be legally registered in Maharashtra and submit applicable documentary evidence.
  • The nominee must also hold the licences, registrations or authorisations applicable to the services or activities entered.
  • The nominated initiative, product, technology, solution or program should have been implemented, commercialised, piloted or demonstrated measurable impact between 1 April 2024 and 31 March 2026. If launched earlier, the nominee must clearly demonstrate significant progress, scale-up, innovation or measurable impact during the eligibility period.

Category-specific experience requirements

Unless stated otherwise, experience is measured as of 31 March 2026.

  • Healthcare Institution of the Year — the participating hospital must have a minimum of 5 completed years of continuous, active healthcare operations.
  • Clinical Excellence Award — the nominee must have a minimum of 5 completed years of clinical practice relevant to the nominated specialty.
  • Nursing Excellence Award — the nominee must have a minimum of 3 completed years of relevant nursing practice.
  • Healthcare Leadership Award — the nominee must have a minimum of 10 completed years of relevant healthcare leadership experience.
  • Yoga Practitioner of the Year — the nominee must have a minimum of 3 completed years of relevant professional yoga practice.
  • Nutrition & Public Health Leadership Award — the nominee must have a minimum of 3 completed years of relevant professional work in nutrition or public health.
  • Excellence in Psychological Care — the nominee must have a minimum of 3 completed years of relevant psychological-care practice.
  • AYUSH Leadership Award — the nominee must have a minimum of 3 completed years of relevant practice or professional work in the nominated AYUSH discipline.
  • Dietitian of the Year — the nominee must have a minimum of 3 completed years of relevant clinical dietetics or clinical nutrition practice.
  • Emerging Hospital of the Year — the nominated organisation must have completed at least 3 years and no more than 5 years of active hospital operations.

Participant eligibility

  • The participant should be 18 years of age to participate in the Awards.
  • An individual nominee should be an Indian citizen currently residing in India, and legally eligible to work in India.
  • An organisation cannot participate on behalf of its sister units, parent organisation or other organisations under the parent organisation.

Governing terms

  • Participation in the awards is subject to the defined terms and conditions.
  • The award categories may be changed, modified, split, merged, increased or cancelled by the Awards Management and/or Jury based on the number of entries received in any category and the quality of the entries. This decision is final, non-contestable and binding on all participants; the Awards management will not entertain queries on this.
  • If at any time, including after the conclusion of the Award ceremony, any information provided by a participant is found to be incorrect in any manner, the participant will be liable to be disqualified and/or required to return the Award.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

General Information

What is Maharashtra Times Swasthya Sangam 2027?

Maharashtra Times Swasthya Sangam 2027 is a healthcare summit, expo, masterclass and awards initiative presented by Maharashtra Times and Times Internet Languages Live. It brings together healthcare professionals, hospitals, industry leaders, innovators and organisations to exchange knowledge, explore advancements and recognise excellence in healthcare.

Who can participate in Swasthya Sangam 2027?

Hospitals, doctors, healthcare professionals, medical institutions, healthcare brands, health-tech companies, startups, medical-device manufacturers and organisations associated with the healthcare and wellness sectors can explore participation opportunities.

Who can submit nominations for the Swasthya Sangam Awards?

Hospitals, doctors, healthcare professionals, healthcare organisations, medical institutions, startups and brands can submit nominations under the relevant award categories, subject to the applicable eligibility requirements.

What are the different opportunities available at Swasthya Sangam?

Swasthya Sangam offers opportunities to participate in the awards, explore exhibition and showcase opportunities, and engage through summit and masterclass-related participation. Organisations can connect with the organising team to understand the available opportunities.

Nomination & Qualification

Is there a fee for submitting nominations?

Yes. Three nomination plans are available — Standard Entry, Spotlight Plan and Elite Entry, each with early-bird and actual pricing exclusive of GST. See the nomination plans above for current pricing and what each plan includes.

What is the difference between the available nomination plans?
  • Standard Entry: One category nomination and listing on the official Awards website.
  • Spotlight Plan: Two category nominations, a digital nomination certificate, social media feature and website listing.
  • Elite Entry: Two category nominations, a digital nomination certificate, social media feature, priority website listing and an exclusive winner video byte/interview.

Can an organisation nominate in multiple award categories?

Yes. The Standard Entry plan includes one category nomination, while the Spotlight and Elite plans include two category nominations. Organisations can select the plan that best suits their nomination requirements.

Does submitting a nomination guarantee an award?

No. All nominations are subject to evaluation. Submitting a nomination does not guarantee an award or recognition. Final recognition is based on the evaluation process.

Judging & Evaluation Process

How are the award winners selected?

Nominations are evaluated based on their relevance, achievements, contributions and merit in the respective award categories. The selection process determines the organisations and individuals recognised for their contributions to the healthcare sector.

Will every organisation that submits a nomination receive an award?

No. Only nominations that meet the evaluation requirements will be considered for recognition. Participation in the nomination process does not guarantee selection as a winner.
