Who can nominate?

Any hospital, doctor, brand or organisation that meets the eligibility rules.

Is there a fee to nominate?

Yes. Each plan lists its fee and what it includes. Prices are exclusive of GST.

How are winners picked?

Does a nomination guarantee an award?

No. Nominations are subject to evaluation, and a nomination is not an assurance of any award or recognition.
